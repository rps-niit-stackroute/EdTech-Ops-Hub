# Your Daily Routine — Time-Blocked

Built around: wake 5:30-6, sleep by 10:30, gym+run combined in the morning, office 9-6, career study split (light review morning / deep work evening). Adjust the exact minutes as you test it — the order and the anchors are what matter.

*Note: this assumes a Mon-Fri office week with Saturday as a flexible/lighter day. If StackRoute has you in on Saturdays, just run the weekday template that day too and shift the Saturday version below to Sunday-only.*

---

## Weekday (Mon-Fri)

The gym commute (25 min each way, 50 min round trip) eats into the morning more than it looks. To keep the light review block *and* still start office by 9:00 without pushing your wake-up earlier than 5:30, the buffer is thinner than before — shower and breakfast are tighter, and there's almost no slack. If gym runs long, trim breakfast, not sleep.

| Time | Block |
|---|---|
| 5:30 | Wake, water, freshen up (10 min) |
| 5:40-5:55 | Light review — flashcards/notes from last night's study, not new material |
| 5:55-6:20 | Commute to gym (25 min) |
| 6:20-7:20 | Gym + run (combined session) |
| 7:20-7:45 | Commute back (25 min) |
| 7:45-8:00 | Shower, get ready |
| 8:00-8:20 | Breakfast |
| 8:20-8:45 | Commute to office (25 min) |
| 8:45-9:00 | Buffer / settle in |
| 9:00-6:00 | Office |
| 6:00-6:30 | Commute back |
| 6:30-7:00 | Decompress — snack, change, 10 min doing nothing on purpose |
| 7:00-8:30 | Deep work block — this is where you do today's row from the 4-Month Career Tracker (SQL, demo practice, applications, referrals) |
| 8:30-9:15 | Dinner |
| 9:15-9:45 | Reading (book, not phone) |
| 9:45-10:15 | Chores / tomorrow's plan — lay out gym clothes, glance at tomorrow's tracker row, quick tidy |
| 10:15-10:30 | Screens off, wind down |
| 10:30 | Sleep |

**Total: ~1 hr workout + 50 min gym commute, ~1.5 hr career study, 30 min reading, 7 hrs sleep, and the rest is office + life admin.**

One honest flag: adding the gym commute drops sleep from 8 hrs to 7 if you hold wake-up at 5:30 and bedtime at 10:30. That's workable but not generous. If mornings feel rough after a week or two, the better fix is moving bedtime to 10:00 rather than cutting the gym commute — 7 hrs is a floor, not a target.

## Saturday (lighter, catch-up day)

| Time | Block |
|---|---|
| 6:00-6:30 | Wake, slower morning |
| 6:30-6:55 | Commute to gym (25 min) |
| 6:55-7:55 | Gym or a longer run — whichever you skipped/rushed this week |
| 7:55-8:20 | Commute back (25 min) |
| 8:20-9:30 | Breakfast + real personal time (no schedule) |
| 9:30-11:30 | Career deep work — this is your buffer slot. Use it to catch up on anything the week's tracker rows didn't finish |
| 11:30-1:30 | Chores, errands, life admin you can't do on weekdays |
| Afternoon | Open — social, rest, whatever you need |
| Evening | Light reading, plan the week ahead, early-ish night |

## Sunday — off, on purpose

Your tracker already marks Sunday as a rest day from career work. Keep it that way. One thing worth doing: 10 minutes on Sunday evening to glance at next week's Daily Plan rows so Monday doesn't start blind. Everything else — sleep in a bit, don't skip the gym entirely if you enjoy it, but don't schedule it.

---

## Why this order, not another

**Workout before study, not after.** Exercise first thing wakes you up faster than caffeine and protects the evening study block from "I'm too tired" — which is usually the actual routine-killer, not lack of time.

**Study is split on purpose.** A 15-minute morning review keeps material from going stale, so the evening block starts from "continuing" instead of "starting cold" — that's why it can be shorter and still work.

**Reading comes after the hard work, not before.** If you put reading first, deep work never happens because reading is easier and you'll linger. Deep work when you have the least resistance left in the day sounds backwards, but it works because the *decision* to start is already made by then — you're just executing the tracker row, not deciding what to do.

---

## Making it actually stick — the part most routines get wrong

**Don't run all seven pieces from day one.** Add gym + one study block this week. Once that's automatic (roughly 2 weeks), add the second study block. Then reading. Trying to install all seven habits simultaneously is the most common reason people abandon a routine in 10 days.

**Protect the anchor, not the exact time.** If gym slips from 6:00 to 6:20 one day, that's fine — the anchor is "gym happens before office," not "gym happens at 6:00 sharp." Rigid to the minute breaks under real life; rigid to the sequence doesn't.

**One weekly check, not daily guilt.** Your career tracker already has a Weekly Review tab and a daily mentor check-in at 8pm. Use Sunday to also ask: did the routine hold, and if not, which block broke first? That's usually your actual bottleneck (commute running long, gym eating into breakfast, etc.) — fix that block specifically instead of rebuilding the whole day.

**Sleep is non-negotiable, not the flexible variable.** If something has to give on a bad day, cut the evening chores block, not sleep. A 10:30 bedtime is what makes 5:30 wake-up sustainable instead of miserable.
